# Conditional Statement Programs

This folder contains beginner-friendly Python programs that demonstrate the use of **conditional statements (`if`, `elif`, and `else`)**. These exercises are designed to help understand decision-making logic in Python.

## Programs

### 1. Age Group Categorization
Classify a person's age group based on their age:
- **Child:** Less than 13
- **Teenager:** 13–19
- **Adult:** 20–59
- **Senior:** 60 and above

---

### 2. Movie Ticket Pricing
Calculate the movie ticket price based on age:
- **Adults (18 and above):** $12
- **Children (Below 18):** $8
- **Wednesday Discount:** Everyone gets a **$2 discount**.

---

### 3. Grade Calculator
Assign a letter grade based on a student's score:
- **A:** 90–100
- **B:** 80–89
- **C:** 70–79
- **D:** 60–69
- **F:** Below 60

---

### 4. Fruit Ripeness Checker
Determine the ripeness of a fruit based on its color.

**Example (Banana):**
- Green → Unripe
- Yellow → Ripe
- Brown → Overripe

---

### 5. Weather Activity Suggestion
Suggest an activity based on the weather:
- **Sunny:** Go for a walk
- **Rainy:** Read a book
- **Snowy:** Build a snowman

---

### 6. Transportation Mode Selection
Choose a mode of transportation based on the distance:
- **Less than 3 km:** Walk
- **3–15 km:** Bike
- **More than 15 km:** Car

---

### 7. Coffee Customization
Customize a coffee order by selecting:
- Coffee size:
  - Small
  - Medium
  - Large
- Optional:
  - Extra shot of espresso

---

### 8. Password Strength Checker
Check the strength of a password based on its length:
- **Weak:** Less than 6 characters
- **Medium:** 6–10 characters
- **Strong:** More than 10 characters

---

### 9. Leap Year Checker
Determine whether a given year is a leap year using the following rules:
- Divisible by **4**
- Not divisible by **100**, unless also divisible by **400**

---

### 10. Pet Food Recommendation
Recommend pet food based on the pet's species and age.

**Examples:**
- Dog (less than 2 years) → Puppy Food
- Dog (more than 5 years) → Senior Dog Food
- Cat (less than 2 years) → Kitten Food
- Cat (more than 5 years) → Senior Cat Food

---

These programs are intended to strengthen the understanding of **Python conditional statements** through simple, real-world examples.
